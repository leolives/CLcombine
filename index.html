How this works

1. Time

Tap an hour, then a minute. No typing.

2. Candles

Tap a candle to select it, then drag gold = close · top grey = high · bottom grey = low. Each candle's open automatically matches the close of the one before it, so all three chain into one continuous price path. Color fills in automatically from close vs. open.

3. Entry, target, stop

Once all 3 candles have been shaped, drag the gold line onto your entry price. Pick Long or Short and the green +$200 target / red -$300 stop lines auto-draw on the correct side, to scale (1 tick = $10).

4. Win / loss

Pick which candle you entered off, then tap +$200 WIN or -$300 LOSS for the outcome.

Data sent

Full OHLC in ticks for all 3 candles, plus entry/target/stop, entry candle, direction, and outcome — sent to your Google Sheet on submit.
